How the ballot works

  • Everyone signs in with their own Face ID, so every star is personal.
  • Star bites and sips in Food, and must-do shows in Activities.
  • Anything with a star lands in Our Gameplan — give picks a time and the night sorts itself into an itinerary.
  • Times are shared: either of you can set or change them, and both phones stay in sync.

Good to know

  • Jazzy Holidays at The Hollywood Brown Derby takes reservations, and they're recommended.
  • The Hollywood Scoops sundaes come in a souvenir cup while supplies last — same for the Tip Top Club's flute drinks.
  • Several favorites are served warm — look for the caramel-apple ciders at Market on a chilly night.
